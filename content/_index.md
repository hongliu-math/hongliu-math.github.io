I am the head of the [Extremal Combinatorics and Probability Group](https://www.ibs.re.kr/ecopro/) (**ECOPRO**) and a distinguished research fellow (首席科学家) at the [Institute for Basic Science](https://www.ibs.re.kr/) (**IBS**). Previously, I was a faculty and a [UKRI Future Leaders Fellow](https://www.ukri.org/our-work/developing-people-and-skills/future-leaders-fellowships/) at the Mathematics Institute at [University of Warwick](http://www.warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/). Before that, I was a [Leverhulme Early Career Fellow](https://www.leverhulme.ac.uk/early-career-fellowships), working with [Oleg Pikhurko](https://homepages.warwick.ac.uk/~maskat/). I got my Ph.D. in 2015 at [University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign](http://www.math.uiuc.edu/), advised by [Józsi Balogh](http://www.math.uiuc.edu/~jobal/).

- I am co-organising the Discrete Math Seminar, the Virtual Discrete Math Colloquium and regular summer/winter schools at IBS. See **[ECOPRO](https://www.ibs.re.kr/ecopro/)** page for announcements.
- Editorial board @ Combinatorica (2026.1-)
- Associate editor @ SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics (2023.1-2025.12)

## Research

I am interested in extremal and probabilistic combinatorics, graph theory, discrete geometry, Ramsey theory, and combinatorial number theory.
